# Demand Letter Template

**Education only — not legal advice.** This template helps you put a dispute in writing. It does not create an attorney-client relationship, and laws vary by state. Consider having a consumer-protection attorney review your letter before sending. Send by certified mail with return receipt requested and keep a copy.
