It is not a court form and does not replace the official forms your court requires. Check your local court's website or clerk's office for exact procedures, deadlines, and forms.

## Part 6 — Before you file

- [ ] Confirm the defendant can be served at the address above
- [ ] Confirm your claim is within your state's small-claims dollar limit
- [ ] Confirm the correct court (where the defendant lives, or where the transaction happened)
- [ ] Try to settle first (send a demand letter — see `templates/demand-letter.md`)
- [ ] Get the court's official complaint form and filing instructions
- [ ] Calculate total cost: filing fee + service of process
- [ ] Decide if you want a lawyer (lawyers are often not allowed in small-claims court; check local rules)
