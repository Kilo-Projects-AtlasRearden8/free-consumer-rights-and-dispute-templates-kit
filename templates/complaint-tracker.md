# Complaint Tracker Template

**Education only — not legal advice.** Use this log to keep track of consumer complaints, who you contacted, what they said, and what happens next. A written record helps if you escalate to a regulator, small-claims court, or an attorney. Store it somewhere safe and back it up.

---

## How to use

- Add one row per complaint.
- Every time you contact the company, add a contact-log entry.
- Keep copies of letters, emails, receipts, and screenshots in a folder named after the complaint.
- Follow up by the dates you set.

## Escalation checklist

When to escalate:
- [ ] No response within 14 days of first contact
- [ ] Company denies a valid warranty claim
- [ ] Charges continue after cancellation
- [ ] Product is unsafe or defective

Where to escalate:
- [ ] Company's executive customer service
- [ ] State attorney general / consumer-protection office
- [ ] Better Business Bureau (BBB)
- [ ] Federal Trade Commission (ReportFraud.ftc.gov)
- [ ] Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (financial products)
- [ ] Small-claims court (see `templates/small-claims-worksheet.md`)
